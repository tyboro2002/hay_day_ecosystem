💡 Why is the Sync Button needed?

Hay Day refreshes the Daily Dirt newspaper every 2 minutes (120 seconds). However, the exact second-mark depends on your individual game session and server connection—there is no fixed global epoch clock for it.

When your mailbox twitches in-game, tap the button above to lock this timer to your live session. It will automatically save your offset and stay synced on every 2-minute loop!
